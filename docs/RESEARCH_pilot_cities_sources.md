# Cue Radar — Pilot Cities Source Candidates (Tel Aviv, Tokyo, Berlin, Vienna, Brussels)

Written 2026-09-17. Scope: candidate `opportunities`/`sources` leads for the 5 cities the owner picked for pilot launch on 2026-09-17 (`docs/PILOT_PLAN.md` Phase 2) — Tel Aviv, Tokyo, Berlin, Vienna, Brussels — to help grow real (non-demo) opportunities from 13 toward the 60+ target, concentrated in these cities.

**This is a candidates list only.** Per `AGENTS.md` rule 5, nothing here has been written to `data/seed/sources.csv` or any Supabase table. Every row still needs the owner to open the institution's real site, confirm current status, and get the direct `apply_url` into the sheet → sync pipeline before it becomes a live `opportunities` row.

**Method:** Live web search plus direct page fetches on 2026-09-17, dated in each entry. "Live call" means I could see, on the institution's own site (or a fetch of it) dated today or very recently, an application window that has not yet closed. "Real institution, not currently live" means the organization and its call-posting behavior are verified real (past or scheduled calls, working domain, named staff/contact) but nothing is open for submission right today. Nothing below is invented — every name has a working URL I actually fetched or that appeared verbatim in search results with a real domain.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Tel Aviv and Vienna both came up with zero confirmed live calls today**, despite having 9 and 8 real institutions respectively. This is a timing/cycle issue, not a "these cities lack institutions" issue — but it means neither city will produce fast, immediate `opportunities` rows the way Berlin/Brussels can right now. Worth deciding whether to (a) wait and re-check these same lists in 3–4 weeks as cycles turn over, or (b) lean on the owner's own local knowledge/network for faster leads in these two cities specifically, per the task's framing.
2. **Tokyo's application culture looks structurally different** — more invitation/curation-based, fewer rolling open submissions — except for Arts Council Tokyo, which is real, public, and has 3 grants closing in about a week (Sept 24–25, 2026). If the owner wants to act on Tokyo fast, that's the one to move on immediately; everything else found is between cycles.
3. **Q-O2's residency deadline needs a same-day human check** — my fetch said "September 15th," which would already be 2 days past as of today; this could be an off-by-a-few-days parsing issue rather than a real closed call, and it's exactly the kind of borderline case the sheet's human-verification step exists for.
4. **City-tagging edge cases carried forward from the prior research**: Israeli Center for Digital Art (Holon, metro Tel Aviv) and Kelim Choreography Center (Bat Yam, adjacent to Tel Aviv) are real and close by but not literally within city limits — same pattern as Kibbutz Contemporary Dance Company in the earlier pass. Worth a single owner decision on how metro-area institutions get tagged, rather than deciding it ad hoc per city.
5. **DAAD Berliner Künstlerprogramm's next window opens in October 2026** — worth flagging as a near-term calendar item even though it's closed today, since "check back in 2 weeks" is actionable in a way "check back in 6 months" isn't.
