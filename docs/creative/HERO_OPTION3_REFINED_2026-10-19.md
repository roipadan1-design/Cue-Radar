# Hero option 3, refined

Creative Director proposal, 2026-10-19. Follows the owner's choice of option 3 in
`HERO_COPY_OPTIONS_2026-10-19.md`. Proposal only; nothing is wired into app code.

Truth flag: open calls and funding are "Soon" in the UI, so the follow-up text puts them in the
future. Profiles are "Coming soon" and are not promised. "The people who run them" refers to
curators and spaces, which are listed.

## 1. Headline

1. **Wherever you land, the work is already happening.** (8 words, stress on "happening")
2. Land anywhere. Someone is already making something. (7 words, two beats, stress on "something")
3. Wherever the work goes, the scene is already there. (9 words, closest to the original)

Top pick: 1. It keeps the road and the living scene, drops the long second clause, and ends on the
verb.

## 2. Follow-up text

Read the bill for your dates in any city: shows, rooms, and the people who run them. Open calls,
residencies and funding are on their way, for the stop after this one. (32 words)

Alternate: Pick a city and your dates. The bill is there, with the rooms and the people behind it.
Open calls, residencies and funding come next. (27 words)

## 3. Punchline (shown very large, max 6 words)

1. **Makers meet makers, city to city.** (covers connection and travel)
2. Same work. New rooms. (travel and arrival, shortest)
3. Makers, rooms, and what comes next. (names all three: people, places, opportunity)
4. Meet the room before you arrive. (arrival, a little practical)

Top pick: 1. It works alone as a brand line and is true today. Option 3 covers opportunity
explicitly but reads like a list.

## 4. Button

1. **Read the bill** (matches the nav name "The bill")
2. Load in (trade term for arriving at a venue; reads as "come in")

Top pick: Read the bill, for consistency with the nav. Neither uses explore, discover, find, browse, see or search.

## Recommended combination, as it reads on the page

>

> residencies and funding are on their way, for the stop after this one.

>
