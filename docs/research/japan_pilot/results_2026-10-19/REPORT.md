## Tokyo scene map

**Dance.** Kagurazaka Session House is the hub: weekday open classes (ballet, contemporary, somatic), a studio theatre that hosts the open-call short-piece platform *Dance Theater 21 Fes* several times a season, the D-ZONE solo/company series, and guest workshops (Mariko Kakizaki's Gaga/people, 20 Oct and 24 Nov; Julie Anne Stanzak in January). Contact improvisation lives in volunteer-run jams such as the Nishiogikubo CI Ws & Jam (1st and 3rd Saturdays). Butoh: Dairakudakan's company season this year was in July (Za-Koenji) and Kobe; nothing found in the window. The international layer arrives through the Tokyo Festival's *Autumn Meteorite* (Okada Toshiki) at Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre — Nadia Beugré's *Burning Girls* (23–25 Oct) is the dance pick.

**Music.** Two poles. **Ftarri** (Suidobashi): a label/shop with 20 seats, improvised and electroacoustic music almost every weekend and many Mondays, constant European visitors; English schedule, email reservation. **FORESTLIMIT** (Hatagaya): a basement where collectives run weeknight live/DJ events and weekend all-nighters (the *Massacre* series, *nannou*, *experienc(e)*); ambient/experimental names like yolabmi and Sofheso play here. **SUPER DOMMUNE** (Shibuya PARCO 9F) streams talks and DJ sets nightly and is a useful radar for who is touring. The **MIMINOIMI Ambient Week** network (spring) is the ambient community's annual focal point — its regulars (e.g. Ayami Suzuki, who plays Ftarri 8–9 Nov) are the people to meet.

**Art.** **Art Center Ongoing** (Kichijoji) — artist-run gallery + bar + library; the most sociable small art space. **BUoY** (Kita-Senju) — artist-run arts centre in a former bowling alley/bathhouse; performance collectives (*RAW PARTY*, with an open section anyone can join), its own NAFAS festival (Sept). **Art Week Tokyo** (4–8 Nov) is institutional but the AWT Bar is where the whole art world drinks.

**Weekly rhythm (observed, not exhaustive).** Ftarri: Sat/Sun and some Mondays at 20:00. Forestlimit: something most nights; weeknights ~18:30–19:00, Fri/Sat parties 22:00–05:00. Session House: classes on weekdays, shows on weekends. Ongoing: Wed–Sun evenings.

**Connections.** Ftarri ↔ Kyoto UrBANGUILD share touring artists (Vittoria Assembri plays UrBANGUILD 12 Nov and Ftarri 15 Nov). Session House ↔ Yokohama Dance Collection alumni (per performer bios). DOMMUNE ↔ MIMINOIMI ↔ Forestlimit form the ambient/electronic triangle.

## Kyoto scene map

**Dance.** **UrBANGUILD** (Kiyamachi) runs *FOuR DANCERS*, a dance-performance night now at vol.364–367 (27 Oct, 4, 13, 26 Nov) with a cheaper "dancer" ticket, plus a monthly *Butoh Night*. **Kyoto Art Center** gives free studios to companies (Monochrome Circus, ANTIBODIES Collective, KIRIKO DANCE PROJECT, Masukawa Kenta…) and runs free monthly *Meirin Workshops* taught by them. **Monochrome Circus** is the contact-improvisation centre of gravity and co-runs the summer **Kyoto International Dance Workshop Festival**. Butoh: Ima Tenko's company **Kiraza**, Masami Yurabe, and Katsura Kan's **Kyoto International Butoh Festival** (7–15 Nov, SPACE LFAN; pre-event 26 Oct at UrBANGUILD).

**Music.** UrBANGUILD again — free improvisation (Umezu Kazutoki/Tada Yoko 10 Nov; Otomo Yoshihide 18 Nov), ambient (Nicolás Melmann + Hirotaka Shirotsubaki 19 Nov), noise (DEAFKIDS 17 Nov), Kukangendai (22 Nov). **Club Metro** (Jingu-Marutamachi) for collective-run club nights; Boro Festa's night session 31 Oct with foodman.

**Art.** @KCUA (Kyoto City University of Arts gallery) and **HAPS** (artist-support office in Higashiyama, with consultations and the *Enjoy Exhibition Club* group gallery walks). Independent Kyoto galleries were not covered in this pass.

**Weekly rhythm.** UrBANGUILD has something most nights at 19:30, then a no-charge bar until midnight — the default after-show hangout. KAC workshops are weekday evenings or weekends.

**Links outward.** Kobe: **DANCE BOX / ArtTheater dB Kobe** (Shin-Nagata, ~1h) is the Kansai dance engine — an 8-month residency, open-class intensives, street performances. Osaka: Kansai bands on UrBANGUILD bills (Weiches Loch) point to an Osaka noise scene not yet mapped. Tokyo: Muni (Tokyo company) shows at KAC 6–8 Nov; Kyoto artists (Kiraza, Zan Yamashita) tour Tokyo rooms.

## How to plug in

- **Take class.** Tokyo: Session House weekday open classes (book on their Airreserve calendar or by phone; English site). Kyoto: KAC's free *Meirin Workshops* (book on teket); ask Monochrome Circus by email about CI classes; DANCE BOX open-class intensive 16–18 Nov (Kobe).
- **Jam.** Nishiogikubo CI jam — no booking, ¥500–1,500, first-timers free on 1st Saturdays (page undated; confirm on their Facebook first).
- **Open stages.** Butoh Festival pre-event at UrBANGUILD on 26 Oct explicitly invites festival dancers to drop in — bring a short solo and ask the organisers before the night. BUoY's *RAW PARTY* has an open section (DM on Instagram). Session House 21 Fes takes 10-minute works by application (from 4 Nov, by phone — a Japanese-speaking friend helps).
- **Where artists hang out after shows.** UrBANGUILD bar (no charge after events, until 24:00). Kyoto Experiment's Festival Share Cafe after marked performances. Art Center Ongoing's bar. AWT Bar during Art Week Tokyo.
- **Ask for help.** HAPS in Kyoto runs a consultation desk for artists (English top page) and group gallery walks — ideal first contact.
- **Etiquette and money.** "予約" (reservation) usually means an email or form with name, date and number of people (Ftarri spells this out; walk-ins pay ¥1,000 more). Small venues add a drink charge (UrBANGUILD: ¥700). Carry cash — Ftarri is cash only. Listings give OPEN (doors) and START; shows start on time. Under-25/29 and "dancer" tickets are common — ask. Photography rules were not verified; ask staff before filming (Kiraza notes its own team films its shows).
